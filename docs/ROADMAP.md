# Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Survey MVP
Projects, XYZ points, CSV, TIN, contours, offline persistence.

## Phase 2 — Mapping
MapLibre, offline tiles, UTM/WGS84, layers and boundaries.

## Phase 3 — RTK
Bluetooth/USB/TCP receivers, NMEA parser, NTRIP/RTCM, FIX/FLOAT status.

## Phase 4 — Grading
Design planes/slopes, cut/fill surfaces, volume reports and guidance UI.

## Phase 5 — Field validation
Calibration, accuracy tests, logging, fail-safe design and controlled pilots.
